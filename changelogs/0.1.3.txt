Realtime is reliable now. Busy apps (whiteboards, cursors, games) no longer fall behind or go quiet: data is batched while the client sends, and arrives in order. Two people starting an app at the same moment always get each other's keys. What arrives while an app is still starting is handed to it when it joins, instead of being lost. Tested with Excalidraw, Realtime Check and Live Chat between Element clients.
